## The line

The queue runs as a line: a drafter, two builders and an inspector, each a fresh agent
per card, with the foreman assigning and merging.

- **Gate.** A card is `low` when it hits nothing on CLAUDE.md's stop-and-ask list (no
  dependency; no schema, contract or public type; no auth, billing or migration; no
  change to an existing test; no lint, CI or agent config) and has at most five files
  and five criteria. The drafter sets a `low` card `ready` itself. Every other card is
  `needs you` and stays `draft` until the owner marks it ready.
- **Agent config** means `.claude/**`, `CLAUDE.md` and anything installed under
  `~/.claude`; those make a card `needs you`. Files under `kit/` are this repo's product,
  not agent config, so a kit card can be `low` (owner, 2026-11-25: 4a is low; 4e stays
  `needs you` because it changes what `/done` does on every card).
- **First commit.** Never a `card/*` branch without a `CARD.md`. When the foreman assigns an
  id, the commit that creates the branch adds a `CARD.md` with the title, `Status: draft`
  and "Next step: being drafted"; the drafter fills it in.
- **Approvals in chat.** When cards wait on the owner, the foreman gives one line listing
  them. The owner answers in one message (e.g. "ready: T-0019, T-0021; hold T-0020
  because …") and the foreman sets the statuses. The owner doesn't edit card files.
- **Runnable checks.** Before a card goes to ready, every criterion's check must be able
  to run on that card's PR. A docs-only PR runs no lint, typecheck or test job (CI's path
  filter skips them), so a docs-only card can't carry a criterion that needs one.
- **Limits.** Draft plus ready at most 5 (owner, 2026-11-25; was 3). In progress at most 2. Inspection at most 3 (owner,
  2026-11-25; was 2); see "Inspection" below.
- **Small questions: decide and log.** If a question is reversible, stays inside the card's
  files in scope, and touches nothing on the stop-and-ask list, the station takes its
  recommended answer, records it under the card's Decisions as "decided for the owner"
  with the alternative, and keeps going. Every such decision is listed in the day-branch
  PR. Only stop-and-ask items and real forks in what the card delivers go to
  `docs/open-questions.md`.
- **Order.** Next up alternates kit cards with shell cards, so the two builders always have
  non-overlapping work; a card's dependents start the moment it merges.
- **Inspection.** Two inspectors, each fresh per card and neither having seen the build;
  the inspection cap is 3. A card waits for `ci` in its own queue and holds no inspection
  slot; an inspector picks it up only once `ci` is green. A red `ci` goes back to its
  builder without being inspected.
- **No waiting on the last card.** Builders don't stop because inspection is full. A
  builder stops only if 3 cards are already waiting for inspection, and then the foreman
  tells the owner. Each merge reports how long the card waited for inspection.
- **Leaving a card.** When a card blocks, its builder commits everything and sets
  "Held by: nobody" before leaving it, so the shell can write to it.
- **Answers.** When an answer lands in a card, the foreman picks it up, sets the card back
  to ready and rewrites `docs/open-questions.md`.
- **Branches (owner, 2026-11-25: a standing `develop` replaces day branches).** Card branches
  are cut from `develop`, their PRs target it, and the foreman merges passed cards into it
  (locally, `--no-ff`). Next up, Parked and `docs/open-questions.md` live on `develop`,
  pushed after every change. A ruleset on `develop` blocks force-push and deletion. `ci`
  runs on every push to `develop`: if it goes red, the foreman stops merging cards, puts a
  card to fix it at the front of Next up, and tells the owner. After every merge the
  foreman checks that `develop` contains `main`; if not, it merges `main` into `develop`
  and tells the owner. Changes flow only `develop` → `main`: the foreman opens that PR when
  the owner asks, with a row per card since the last one and the "Closes #N" lines. The
  owner merges it. `day/2026-11-25` stays until its contents are on `main`.
- **Builders push as they go.** A builder commits and pushes its card branch after every
  meaningful step, not only at the end, so the shell sees work in progress.
- A held or blocked card leaves the line with its reason in the card. If two cards turn
  out to touch the same file, the later one goes back to ready.

The slop list:

- comments that restate the code, or narrate the change ("added", "now we", "fix")
- try/catch that only logs, rethrows or swallows
- null and type checks on values the types already guarantee
- a helper, wrapper or abstraction used once
- unused exports, parameters, variables and branches; leftover debug logging
- `any`, casts and suppressions used to quiet the type checker or linter
- logic that duplicates an existing utility (search before writing)
- fallbacks, flags and compatibility shims nobody asked for
- renaming or reformatting lines the card didn't need to touch
- a new dependency for something small
- tests that assert the mock or the implementation, not the behaviour
- docs, READMEs, banners and emoji nobody asked for

## Done branches show as done

Front of the line, `low` (owner, 2026-11-25). A `card/*` branch with no readable `CARD.md`
is one of three things:

- **done:** its id is in `cards/done/` on the default or day branch. Show under Done.

  done. Only this one is "unreadable", and only this one notifies.

Notifications fire for exactly four things: a question to answer, a `needs you` card to
approve, a held card, and a broken card. Nothing else.
